More than just a ride.

Fetch Mobility offers you more than just a ride! We are dedicated to revolutionizing the way people move around by offering effortless, sustainable, and accessible transportation solutions with our smart electric trikes.

At Fetch Mobility, we believe everyone deserves easy and affordable access to transportation.

Whether you are a student, staff member, professor or IT professional, Fetch scooters are designed and built for all. Fetch is available for anyone on campus, ensuring all can move freely, comfortably and independently. But that’s not all. With Fetch, we’re not just helping you move—we’re helping shape a smarter, more sustainable world, one ride at a time.
